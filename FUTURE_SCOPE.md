# PitchProof — Future Scope & Upcoming Capabilities

A prioritized, concise roadmap of high-impact features and architectural additions for PitchProof.

---

## 1. New Specialized Agents
- **Agent 7: Go-To-Market (GTM) & Distribution Strategy**
  - Tailored customer acquisition channels (SEO, developer evangelism, cold outbound B2B, viral loops).
  - High-converting launch copy, cold email templates, and landing page value proposition variants.
- **Agent 8: Interactive Founder AI Copilot**
  - Multi-turn conversational chat directly grounded in the idea's validation dossier.
  - Answers targeted questions (*"What pricing tiers should I test?"*, *"How do I counter Competitor X's enterprise moat?"*).
- **Agent 9: Pitch Deck & Financial Economics Modeler**
  - 10-slide investor pitch deck outline (Problem, Solution, TAM/SAM/SOM, Moat, Business Model, Team, Milestones).
  - Unit economics simulator (CAC, LTV, payback period, and gross margin projections).

---

## 2. Autonomous Market Watchdogs & Continuous Intelligence
- **Trend Spike Alerts**: Periodic background polling of Google Trends and news feeds; automated notifications when market demand surges.
- **Customer Pain Point Mining**: Live scraping of complaints and unmet demand from Reddit (e.g., r/startups, r/SaaS), Product Hunt, and G2.
- **Regulatory & Compliance Clearance**: Automated screening for required licenses and certifications (HIPAA, GDPR, SOC2, FINRA).

---

## 3. Collaboration, Sharing & Exports
- **Shareable Public Dossier Links**: Tokenized read-only links for sharing reports with co-founders and investors.
- **Native PDF & Slide Export**: One-click generation of styled PDF dossiers and presentation decks.
- **Team & Workspace Mode**: Multi-user workspaces for startup accelerators, venture studios, and hackathons.
